Vintage Cookbook Engraving — Master Illustration Prompt

[SUBJECT]

Create a vintage mid-century cookbook illustration of [SUBJECT], composed in the exact visual language of a traditional printed culinary engraving.

Style

* Pure black-and-white pen-and-ink engraving / etching
* Realistic hand-drawn culinary illustration
* Vintage 1940s–1960s cookbook aesthetic
* Fine engraved linework
* Dense parallel hatching
* Delicate cross-hatching
* Stippling and dot shading
* Etched contours and carefully varied line weight
* Small irregularities characteristic of traditional hand-printed illustration
* Clearly visible printed ink / paper grain texture
* Detailed but restrained, instructional rather than decorative
* Natural, believable food forms
* Strong dimensionality created entirely through black ink density and white space
* Off-white / cream paper background only

Palette

STRICTLY MONOCHROME.

* Black ink
* White / off-white paper

No colored ink whatsoever.

The apparent tonal range must come entirely from:

* black linework
* cross-hatching
* stippling
* engraving density
* white negative space
* subtle off-white paper texture

Composition

* 4:3 aspect ratio
* Subject centered
* Slight three-quarter view where appropriate
* Generous clean negative space surrounding the subject
* Balanced, calm cookbook composition
* Natural tabletop / prop arrangement when appropriate
* Avoid excessive decoration
* Keep the main subject immediately recognizable
* Props should remain secondary to the main subject

Texture & Rendering

Use authentic-looking vintage engraving techniques rather than modern digital illustration.

Surfaces should be described through:

* fine parallel strokes
* cross-hatched shadows
* stippled tonal areas
* etched outlines
* small imperfections in the printed ink
* subtle paper grain

Do not use smooth digital gradients.
Do not use flat fills.
Do not make the illustration look like a vector drawing.

Important Negative Prompt

NO COLOR WHATSOEVER.

No brown, gold, yellow, orange, red, purple, blue, navy, green, lime, beige, or colored accents.

No photorealism.
No digital painting.
No watercolor.
No gouache.
No flat vector illustration.
No modern cartoon style.
No 3D rendering.
No glossy CGI.
No photographic lighting.
No text.
No labels.
No typography.
No borders.
No people unless explicitly requested.

The result must look like a real vintage black-ink cookbook engraving printed on slightly warm/off-white paper.

Output

A highly detailed but elegant black-and-white vintage cookbook engraving, with the subject clearly readable at first glance, authentic etched linework and stippling, subtle printed grain, strong use of negative space, and a restrained mid-century culinary illustration aesthetic.
