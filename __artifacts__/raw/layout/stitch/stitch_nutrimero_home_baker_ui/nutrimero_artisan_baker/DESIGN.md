## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies the quiet, capable warmth of a professional bakery production room run from a home studio. It balances the exacting precision of formulation chemistry with the grounded, sensory calm of an organized kitchen pantry. It avoids the sterility of SaaS dashboards and the performative gloss of lifestyle magazines, presenting instead as an authoritative, well-used physical ledger: disciplined, tactile, and clear.

The design movement is **Tactile-Editorial Utility**. The interface feels printed and structured, utilizing warm paper-like containers, crisp borders, and monoline structural details that recall flour-dusted notebook margins, stamped provenance labels, and production tally sheets. 

### Visual Tenets
- **Pantry Architecture**: Content is compartmentalized cleanly into tonal shelves and structural cells rather than floating in ambiguous space.
- **Formulation Authority**: Quantities, percentages, allergens, and yields are presented with the quiet confidence of a master baker's notebook—scannable, unambiguous, and tabular.
- **Disciplined Energy**: The vivid Acid Lime accent is strictly functional, acting as an intentional kitchen timer or priority tag amidst warm stone and deep navy surfaces.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in high-contrast utility anchored by domestic warmth. 

### Light Mode Architecture
- **Primary (`#1b1f58`)**: Deep Navy. Serves as the structural anchor, primary text color, high-emphasis icons, and structural divider rules.
- **Secondary (`#b9bf05`)**: Acid Lime. Reserved strictly for primary calls-to-action, focus indicators, and key operational states. Any text placed upon an Acid Lime background **must** use Near-Black Olive (`#1d1e01`). White text on Acid Lime is strictly prohibited under all circumstances to ensure AA/AAA legibility.
- **Tertiary (`#2f0100`)**: Oxblood. Dedicated to critical allergen disclosures, safety parameters, temperature limits, and warning badges. Always pair with an explicit icon glyph for accessible color-blind redundancy.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**:
  - Base Screen Background: `#fbf9f4` (Milled Flour Cream)
  - Card & Interactive Surface: `#ffffff` (Pure Surface)
  - Surface-Container: `#f3efe6` (Kitchen Linen)
  - Surface-Tonal: `#ede8dc` (Unbleached Parchment)
  - Illustration Canvas: `#f7f4ec` (Editorial Canvas)
- **Slate Neutrals**:
  - Text Secondary / Inactive: `#334155` (Slate Charcoal)
  - Borders / Dividers / Neutral Icons: `#64748b` (Muted Slate)
  - Recessed Well Fills: `#f1f5f9` (Cool Flour)

### Dark Mode Architecture
- **Screen Background**: `#0f122e` (Midnight Oven)
- **Surface**: `#171b40` (Tempered Navy)
- **Surface Container**: `#1f2452` (Deep Pantry)
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#282f6a` (Structural Indigo)
- **Primary Text**: `#f3efe6` (Warm Flour)
- **Secondary Text**: `#94a3b8` (Slate Mist)
- **Accent**: `#b9bf05` retains its vivid role with `#1d1e01` labels.

## Typography

The design system exclusively uses **Plus Jakarta Sans**, harnessing its geometry and human warmth to evoke modern culinary discipline.

### Numerical & Tabular Data Rule
Baker's percentages, ingredient weights, temperature targets, hydration ratios, and costings must be rendered using tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;` or React Native `fontVariant: ['tabular-nums']`). This prevents visual shifting during live batch adjustments and ensures data aligns along exact decimal spines.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Editorial Section Headers (`headline-lg` / `headline-lg-mobile`)**: Used for primary formula names and dashboard overviews. Kept tight to prevent awkward wraps on compact devices.
- **Metric Grouping (`numeric-metric`, `numeric-data`)**: Dedicated to ingredient quantities, cost-per-loaf metrics, and bake timers. Paired with `label-sm` in all-caps for field descriptions (e.g., `TOTAL DOUGH WT`, `BAKER'S %`).
- **Allergen Warnings**: Must always combine `label-md` or `label-lg` with uppercase styling to guarantee visual hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout is structured around a strict 4px grid rhythm, optimized for rapid, one-handed mobile kitchen operations (floury thumbs, dynamic standing view).

### Layout Geometry
- **Screen Margins**: Fixed `16px` (`margin`) outer horizontal margins on mobile devices to preserve horizontal real estate for dense batch sheets and recipe rows.
- **Gutters**: `12px` (`gutter`) between interior two-column metric cards and provenance blocks.
- **Component Touch Target Minimum**: `44px` physical touch bounds across all interactives, even when optical bounds are smaller (such as quantity steppers or batch multiplier chips).

### Ergonomic Thumb-First Hierarchy
Primary action anchors (e.g., "Start Mix Timer", "Scale Batch", "Log Bake") sit pinned to bottom sheets or thumb-reachable bottom docks (`space-md` above screen safe areas). High-density metric verification rests in the middle eye-level zone, while non-interactive recipe metadata remains safely out of reach at the top scroll realm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tonal Layering with Crisp Structural Rules** instead of heavy shadows. Baking environments demand instant readability without blurred visual artifacts.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Screen Floor)**: Screen base background (`#fbf9f4`). The ambient workspace.
- **Level 1 (Shelf & Structural Surfaces)**: Container cards and ingredient ledgers rendered in `#ffffff` or `#f3efe6`, bounded by a subtle `1px` stroke in `#ede8dc` (Dark mode: `#282f6a`).
- **Level 2 (Active Tool Surfaces & Modals)**: Bottom sheets, timer drawers, and floating unit pickers rendered in `#ffffff`, separated with an ambient micro-shadow: `offset: (0, 2), blur: 8, color: #1b1f58, opacity: 0.06`.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Focus & Popovers)**: Provenance callouts and urgent warnings utilize an explicit `1.5px` border in `#1b1f58` or `#2f0100` alongside an offset physical drop: `offset: (2, 2), blur: 0, color: #1b1f58` for a crisp, physical stamp aesthetic.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is **Soft-Technical**. Forms are calibrated to feel like cleanly machined kitchen rulers, silicone spatulas, and precision weights—deliberate, grounded, and never childishly pill-shaped or bubbly.

### Geometry Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Steppers)**: `4px` corner radii. Delivers a tactile, durable feeling.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges**: `6px` to `8px` corner radii.
- **Cards & Data Wells**: Max `12px` corner radii. Larger radiuses waste vital data padding and degrade tabular alignment.
- **Stamps & Provenance Badges**: Distinctive notched or `2px` micro-radius corners with dashed or double-stroke inner borders to simulate physical artisan flour-bag certification stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (The "Bake / Commit" Button)**:
  - Height: `48px`, radius: `8px`.
  - Fill: Acid Lime (`#b9bf05`).
  - Text: `label-lg`, color: Near-Black Olive (`#1d1e01`). Weight: `700`. Never Navy, never White.
  - Active State: Background drops 8% luminance to `#a3a804`.
- **Secondary Action (Formulation / Adjust)**:
  - Fill: Transparent. Border: `1.5px` Deep Navy (`#1b1f58`).
  - Text: Deep Navy (`#1b1f58`), `label-lg`.
- **Danger / Critical Override**:
  - Fill: Oxblood (`#2f0100`), Text: Cream Canvas (`#f7f4ec`), radius: `8px`.

### Chips & Provenance Badges
- **Status / Hydration Chips**:
  - Height: `28px`, radius: `4px`.
  - Fill: `#f3efe6`, border: `1px` solid `#64748b` (30% opacity).
  - Text: `label-sm`, Deep Navy (`#1b1f58`).
- **Provenance Badges**:
  - Physical bakery certification tags (e.g., "Stoneground T80", "100% Levain").
  - Fill: `#f7f4ec` Canvas. Inset border: `1px` dashed `#1b1f58`. Monoline wheat or mill glyph on left. Text: `10px` uppercase bold.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Ingredient Weight Fields**:
  - Height: `52px`, background: `#ffffff`, border: `1.5px` solid `#ede8dc`.
  - Focus State: Border switches directly to Deep Navy (`#1b1f58`) with an inner 1px Acid Lime focus ring.
  - Value: `numeric-metric` using tabular figures. Trailing unit affixed inside right container (e.g., `g`, `kg`, `%`) in `#334155`.
- **Touch Steppers (- / +)**:
  - Square touch surfaces (`44px` x `44px`) hugging input borders, providing distinct physical click states for mid-mix adjustments without typing.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Recipe and ingredient lists are presented as ledger lines.
- Height: Minimum `48px` row spacing.
- Bottom divider: `1px` hairline in `#ede8dc` (Dark mode: `#282f6a`).
- Columnar layout: Ingredient Name (flex-1, `body-md`, `#1b1f58`) -> Percentage (`body-sm`, `#64748b`, tabular) -> Weight (`numeric-data`, `#1b1f58`, tabular).

### Allergen & Warning Cards
- Background: Tinted parchment with a `4px` solid Oxblood (`#2f0100`) left indicator spine.
- Leading icon: Mandatory warning triangle or grain cross glyph in `#2f0100`.
- Text: Oxblood headline (`label-md`) with high-contrast neutral body (`#334155`).

### Editorial Flat Illustration Rules
- Spot illustrations (proofing bowls, scoring blades, dutch ovens) utilize warm Terracotta (`#c25e42`), Wild Honey (`#d99b26`), and Deep Navy line work (`#1b1f58`, stroke width `1.5px`).
- Background canvas for illustrations is always Cream Canvas (`#f7f4ec`).
- Acid Lime is used strictly as an accent point (e.g., steam highlight, scoring marker, timer ring tick).
